biaser

Ambient music that exists once and never repeats.

22 sound layers 5 mood states Zero saved state
YouTube demo (4–9 min) - insert embed link

biaser generates ambient music through phase-shifting loops. The concept draws on generative music principles: sound environments that assemble themselves organically without a predetermined sequence.

The system runs on multiple short audio loops playing simultaneously, constantly drifting out of sync due to coprime loop durations.

Architecture & Implementation

5 mood modes, each drawing from a pool of 16 pre-built sound configurations. Each configuration manages 22 simultaneous audio channels running loop lengths between 18 and 50 seconds to maximize phase variation before repetition.

Frustrator

An interactive soundscape exploring non-linear control loops.

6 audio modules 6–11 parameters each Dynamic parameter shuffling

Frustrator is an interactive audio application built around delayed feedback and stochastic control mechanics.

How it works

Six audio modules manage between 6 to 11 live control parameters. Upon application startup, parameters are assigned randomized baseline values and mapping targets.
