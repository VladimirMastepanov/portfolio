biaser

Ambient music that exists once and never repeats.

22 sound layers 5 mood states Zero saved state
Watch more biaser demos on YouTube

biaser generates ambient music through phase-shifting loops. The idea draws on generative music principles: sound environments that assemble themselves organically, with no fixed sequence behind them.

The system runs several short audio loops at once, and because their durations are coprime, they keep drifting out of sync with each other.

Architecture and implementation

There are 5 mood modes, each pulling from a pool of 16 pre-built sound configurations. A configuration runs 22 audio channels simultaneously, with loop lengths between 18 and 50 seconds chosen to delay repetition as long as possible.

Frustrator

An interactive soundscape exploring non-linear control loops.

6 audio modules 6-11 parameters each Dynamic parameter shuffling
Watch more Frustrator demos on YouTube

Frustrator is an interactive audio application built around delayed feedback and stochastic control mechanics.

How it works

Six audio modules manage between 6 and 11 live control parameters. On launch, every parameter gets a randomized baseline value and mapping target.
